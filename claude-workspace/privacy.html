Privacy policy

Last updated: 27 September 2026.

Claude Workspace is a personal tool used only by its owner, Adrian Turbiński (adrian.turbinski@gmail.com). It is not available to other users.

Data accessed

With the owner's consent, the tool accesses the owner's Gmail messages, labels and filters and the owner's Google Calendar events through the Google APIs.

How data is used

Data is used only to organize the owner's mailbox (labels, archiving, moving to trash, filters) and to create or update calendar events at the owner's request. Access tokens are stored only on the owner's computer.

Sharing

Data is not sold, not shared with third parties and not used for advertising. Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
